# kith_game

An original creature-collector built on one conceit: **a story told often enough grows a body.**

Creatures are not animals. They are local legend made flesh, and belief is the thing that keeps them alive. You bind one by learning its story and speaking its true name. Every region reads the world through its own cosmology, so a creature carried across a border is reinterpreted by the people who live there — different typing, different matchups, different moves.

Tell a story too widely and it stops being a story. The land starts agreeing with it, and people have to live in the world you talked into existence.

## Status

Pre-production. Design only — no code yet.

The next concrete step is a grey-box prototype: one valley, two cosmologies, six creatures, colored cubes for models. The single question it has to answer is whether watching a myth saturate and a region transform actually produces a feeling. Everything else waits on that.

## Core systems at a glance

- **Binding** — capture replaced by collecting a myth's verses and naming it. Rarity is obscurity, not RNG.
- **Cosmologies** — no universal type chart; each region re-reads a creature through its own system.
- **The belief ledger** — belief is the world-state variable, zero-sum within a region.
- **Bleed** — an over-believed myth transmutes terrain, then empties it of the people who carried it.
- **Syncretism** — two saturated myths in one place fuse, at a cost.
- **Rumors** — deliberately false stories produce rootless, untranslatable, parasitic creatures.
- **The Messenger** — a late reveal about who the player actually is.
